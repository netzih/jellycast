JellyCast Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

JellyCast does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It is a personal music client that connects only to a Jellyfin media server you configure and to Google Cast speakers on your local network.

What stays on your device

Your server address and your Jellyfin access token are stored on your device only — the token in secure system storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore), your preferences in local app storage. They are never transmitted anywhere except to the Jellyfin server you chose.

No analytics, no tracking

JellyCast contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Google Cast

To send audio to a Cast speaker, the bundled Google Cast SDK may process a device identifier and diagnostic data. This is used solely to enable casting, is not linked to your identity, and is not used to track you. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
